About the Academy

A dedicated home for serious cricket, in Madipakkam

AJVJ Cricket Academy is a training facility in Madipakkam, Chennai, dedicated to grooming the next generation of cricketing talent. Led by coach Ajaay Shankar, we provide structured, professional coaching for kids, teenagers and aspiring league players.

Our curriculum covers batting, bowling and fielding technique, alongside physical fitness, strength conditioning and game awareness — with professional practice nets and turf pitches giving students hands-on match and tournament exposure to build real on-field confidence.

Coaching

Every part of the game, covered

A curriculum built to take a player from first-ball nerves to league-ready composure.

B

Batting Technique

Stance, footwork and shot selection, built from correct fundamentals up to advanced strokeplay against pace and spin.

B

Bowling Craft

Pace, seam and spin fundamentals, with control, line and variation developed through focused, repeated drilling.

F

Fielding & Athleticism

Catching, ground fielding and throwing accuracy, sharpened with reaction speed and agility conditioning.

S

Strength & Conditioning

Physical fitness training built specifically for the movement patterns and load of match cricket.

G

Game Awareness

Reading situations, field placements and match strategy — the thinking side of the game, taught alongside the physical.

T

Tournament Exposure

Real match and tournament play, so confidence is built where it matters — out on the field, not just in the nets.
